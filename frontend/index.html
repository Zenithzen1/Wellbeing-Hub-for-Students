The waitlist

If every counsellor is booked for the next seven days, join a line. When someone cancels, the first student in line gets the time and 30 minutes to take it. Waiting more than 72 hours moves you up.

Each student books under a tag

Your email becomes a fixed tag through HMAC-SHA256, so a counsellor sees #47C32E and the reason you picked. A name you choose to share is sealed with a key only the custodian holds.

  • Your name goes to one counsellor, for one session

Questions

About booking, names and the waitlist.

Email the counselling team

Who can see my name on a booking?

Nobody, unless you switch on Share my name for a session. Then that counsellor sees it for that session only. Everyone else sees a tag such as #47C32E.

How long is a session?

45 minutes, or 60 for your first visit. Sessions start on the quarter hour, and you can book up to 60 days ahead.

What if no session is free this week?

Join one counsellor’s line or the line for any counsellor. Mark it urgent if you need someone within 48 hours; urgent requests are offered time first.

What if I miss a waitlist offer?

The time goes to the next student and you keep your place. Miss a second offer and you leave that line.

Can I cancel a session?

Yes, from My sessions. The time is offered to the waitlist before anyone else can book it.
